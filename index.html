01 — Our philosophy

Food that feels
like coming home.

Punjabi Tadka Kitchen is more than a meal. It's a celebration of the flavours, traditions and hospitality of Punjab.

Our approach starts with quality ingredients, time-honoured cooking and spices that bring every dish to life. We aim to choose locally sourced and organic ingredients wherever possible, while keeping the focus on generous flavour and a welcoming experience.
