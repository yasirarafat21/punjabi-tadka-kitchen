# Assessment Guide — Punjabi Tadka Kitchen

## How the site aligns to the assessment outline

1. **Introduction, subject area and market background (5 marks)**
   - Present the restaurant concept as Punjabi food, culture and hospitality in the UK.
   - In the presentation, explain the intended audience (families, students, friends and people interested in Indian/Punjabi food), based on the uploaded presentation.
   - Add external market research only if required, citing any sources used.

2. **Website structure and topic justification (10 marks)**
   - Homepage hero and brand proposition.
   - Our philosophy, menu, experience, food journal, newsletter and contact sections.
   - The topic uses the uploaded presentation's message: “Taste Punjab. Feel Punjab. Enjoy Punjab.”

3. **Primary activities, digital marketing mix and media (15 marks)**
   - Primary activities: showcase menu items, communicate food philosophy, publish food stories, encourage enquiries and build an opt-in audience.
   - Digital marketing mix: product = Punjabi menu and hospitality; price = not yet published and must be confirmed; place = UK-focused concept but actual location must be added; promotion = SEO, social content, email and future paid campaigns.
   - Media: food photography, short recipe/culture videos, menu cards, article images and social posts.

4. **SEO, social media, future PPC and affiliate marketing (15 marks)**
   - SEO already included: descriptive page title, meta description, keyword metadata, heading hierarchy, alt text, internal links, Open Graph metadata and Restaurant structured data.
   - Social strategy: Instagram/TikTok short food videos, Facebook community updates and Google Business Profile if there is a real eligible location.
   - PPC: propose a small local search/social pilot only after location, audience and conversion tracking are set up.
   - Affiliate marketing: optional partnerships with local food creators or relevant food products; disclose paid/affiliate relationships.
   - Do not claim SEO rankings, ad results or partnerships until they have actually occurred.

5. **Analytics, feedback, data analysis and improvements (30 marks)**
   - Connect Google Analytics 4 or another privacy-appropriate analytics tool after publication, with consent controls where legally required.
   - Track: sessions/users, traffic source, menu section engagement, journal views, CTA clicks, form starts/submissions and newsletter sign-up attempts.
   - Use Search Console to review impressions, clicks, queries and indexing after verification.
   - Collect feedback with a short survey or genuine customer feedback; avoid inventing reviews.
   - Improvement loop: identify the weakest page/CTA, propose a change, measure the same metric over a defined period and compare before/after.
   - The website currently contains no real visitor analytics. Use screenshots of your own live analytics after data has been collected. For a short assessment, clearly label any sample/mock dashboard as illustrative rather than actual performance.

6. **Social media/content aesthetics, development skills and class interaction (20 marks)**
   - Show responsive design on desktop and mobile.
   - Demonstrate menu filters/search, mobile navigation and form validation.
   - Discuss brand consistency: warm cream, deep green, saffron/orange, serif headlines and food imagery.
   - Include actual feedback from peers/tutor and explain what you changed. Do not fabricate interaction or feedback.

7. **Conclusion and future developments (5 marks)**
   - Future work: real booking/order integration, verified location and opening hours, backend forms, accessibility testing, analytics, customer reviews with permission, multilingual content and online ordering.

## Evidence checklist
- Screenshot of homepage on desktop and mobile.
- Screenshots showing menu category filters and search working.
- Screenshot of SEO metadata / page title, and structured data validation if completed.
- Published URL and date.
- Analytics setup screenshot and real collected data, or clearly marked “not yet available”.
- Social media content calendar and sample posts.
- Short record of peer/tutor feedback and changes made.
- YouTube presentation URL and final slide/notes.

## Suggested YouTube presentation (about 5–7 minutes)
1. 0:00–0:30 — Introduce Punjabi Tadka Kitchen and the target audience.
2. 0:30–1:15 — Explain topic choice, brand proposition and site map.
3. 1:15–2:30 — Live walkthrough: homepage, story, menu filter/search, journal and contact section.
4. 2:30–3:30 — Explain SEO foundations and social media/content plan.
5. 3:30–4:45 — Explain analytics plan, metrics, feedback and improvement cycle. Show only real analytics evidence; otherwise state tracking is awaiting data.
6. 4:45–5:30 — Discuss digital marketing mix, proposed PPC/affiliate activity and risks/limitations.
7. 5:30–6:00 — Summarise findings and future developments.

## Publishing steps
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Preview `index.html` locally and test all links/forms/menu filters.
3. Replace placeholder email and any details that are not verified.
4. Publish the folder using Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages.
5. Test mobile layout, keyboard navigation, image loading, page title and contact details on the live URL.
6. Set up analytics and consent/privacy information appropriate to your audience and jurisdiction.
7. Add the final live website URL and YouTube URL to your assessment template.
